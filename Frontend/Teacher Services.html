Teacher Services

Teacher Services provide students with a safe and school-monitored platform to communicate with their teachers, ask academic questions, receive guidance, and request help whenever needed.

  • Subject Teachers
  • Contact Teacher

Contact Teacher

Available Services

Service Description
Class Teachers View information and contact your class teacher.
Subject Teachers Contact teachers for specific subjects.
Contact Teacher Send academic questions or request guidance.
Live Chat Real-time chat with teachers during school hours.
Message Teacher Send private messages that teachers can reply to later.
Ask for Help Request extra academic support or learning assistance.

Safety Notice

  • All chats and messages are securely stored by the school system.
  • Communication is monitored by school administration for student safety.
  • Students can only contact teachers assigned to their class or subjects.
  • Teachers can reply during school working hours.
  • Parents and school administration can review communication if required.
