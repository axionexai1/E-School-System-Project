AI Study Assistant

The AI Study Assistant helps students improve their learning by answering questions based only on the school's uploaded study material and providing personalized academic guidance.

AI Services

  • Study Tips
  • Attendance Analysis
  • Performance Analysis
  • Weak Subjects
  • Improvement Suggestions
  • Personalized Recommendations

Ask AI Assistant

AI Assistant Rules

  • Answers only questions related to the school's uploaded course material.
  • Does not answer unrelated topics such as entertainment or personal advice.
  • Never shares another student's information, grades, or personal data.
  • Protects every student's privacy and follows school safety policies.
  • Provides educational assistance only.

AI Learning Analysis

AI Feature Description
Study Tips Daily study suggestions based on current subjects.
Attendance Analysis Reviews attendance percentage and warns if attendance is low.
Performance Analysis Analyzes exam and test results to identify learning progress.
Weak Subjects Identifies subjects that need more attention.
Improvement Suggestions Provides practical advice for improving academic performance.
Personalized Recommendations Suggests study plans based on the student's academic history.

Example AI Response

"Your attendance is 82%. Your Mathematics marks are below average. Study Algebra for 30 minutes daily and complete your pending assignments this week."
