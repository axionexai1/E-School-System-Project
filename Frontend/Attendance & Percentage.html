Attendance Information

  • Daily Attendance: Shows Present, Absent, or Leave for each day.
  • Monthly Attendance: Displays attendance in a calendar view for each month.
  • Annual Attendance: Shows a complete yearly attendance summary.
  • Attendance Percentage: Displays the student's overall attendance percentage.
  • Attendance History: Shows attendance records from Nursery to the current class.
