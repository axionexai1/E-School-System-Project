Complete Fee History

This section stores the student's complete fee history from the admission date until the current academic year. No payment record is deleted, allowing students and parents to review previous payments whenever required.

Fee Details

Fee Type Description
Admission Fee One-time fee paid when the student is admitted.
Monthly Fee Regular tuition fee collected every month.
Annual Fee Fee charged once every academic year.
Challan History Displays every challan generated for the student.
Payment Status Clearly shows whether the fee is Paid or Pending, along with the Due Date.
