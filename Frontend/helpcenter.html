Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I reset my password?

    Go to Settings and select "Change Password" to reset it securely.

  • How can I check my attendance?

    Open the Attendance & Percentage card from the dashboard.

  • Who do I contact for fee issues?

    Use the Contact with School page to reach the accounts office.

  • How do I message a teacher?

    Open the Contact with Teacher card and select the teacher you want to message.
