Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Go to Settings in the left sidebar and select "Change Password" to update your account password securely. If you are locked out, contact the school IT desk.

How can I check my attendance?

Open the Attendance & Percentage card directly from your main Dashboard to view daily attendance records and monthly percentage breakdowns.

Who do I contact for fee or challan issues?

Visit the Contact with School page to reach the Accounts & Fee Office directly via phone, email, or the inquiry form.

How do I message a teacher or apply for leave?

Use the Contact with Teacher card on the Dashboard to message your subject teacher, or open Online Applications to submit a formal leave request online.

How do I correct a mistake in my profile details?

Student profile records are read-only for security. Please have your parent or guardian submit a correction request through the school administration office.
