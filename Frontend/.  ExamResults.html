Exam Results

Shows results for all examinations the student has taken, including results from previous academic years.

Result Categories

  • First Term
  • Mid Term
  • Final Term
  • Previous Classes Results
  • Subject Wise
  • Overall Result
  • Grade
  • Percentage

Each Result Record Contains

Field Description
Subject Name of the subject.
Obtained Marks Marks obtained by the student.
Total Marks Maximum marks for the subject.
Grade Grade awarded according to the marking scheme.
Percentage Overall percentage calculated from total marks.
Result Status Displays Pass or Fail status.

Complete Academic History

This section stores the student's complete academic history, allowing students and parents to view examination results from Nursery to the current class.

  • View results from previous classes.
  • Check subject-wise performance.
  • View overall percentage and grade.
  • Compare performance across academic years.
  • Download or print result cards.
