Privacy Policy

This policy explains how student information is collected, used, and protected within the Student Portal.

Overview

The school is committed to protecting the privacy of students and their families. This policy describes what information is collected and how it is safeguarded.

Information We Collect

  • Student name, class, and roll number.
  • Attendance, grades, and academic records.
  • Parent or guardian contact information.
  • Fee and payment history.

How We Use Information

  • To manage academic records and communication.
  • To process fee payments and generate challans.
  • To notify parents of school updates and announcements.

Data Security

  • Student data is stored on secure, access-controlled systems.
  • Only authorized school staff can access personal records.
  • Information is never shared with third parties without consent.

Questions About This Policy

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out through the Contact with School page.
