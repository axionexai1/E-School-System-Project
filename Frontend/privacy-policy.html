Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect student information

  • Announcements 0
Back to Dashboard

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview

The school is committed to protecting the privacy of students and their families. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected within the Student Management System Portal, how it is used, and how it is kept secure.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and maintain only the information required for academic and administrative operations:

  • Student name, admission number, class, section, and roll number.
  • Attendance records, examination marks, grades, and academic history.
  • Parent or guardian contact information and residential address.
  • Fee challans and payment history.

3. How We Use Information

  • To manage student academic records, report cards, and class schedules.
  • To process fee payments and generate official challans.
  • To notify parents and students about circulars, holidays, and school announcements.
  • To contact guardians in case of emergencies.

4. Data Security & Confidentiality

  • All student records are stored on secure, access-controlled school systems.
  • Only authorized school administrators and teachers can access personal records.
  • Student information is never sold or shared with third parties without parent or guardian consent.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request a correction to your records, please reach out through our Contact with School page.
