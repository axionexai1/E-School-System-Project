1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and using this Student Management System Portal, you agree to comply with these Terms & Conditions as well as all applicable school policies and academic rules.

2. User Responsibilities

All students and parents using the portal are expected to follow these guidelines:

  • Keep your login credentials and password strictly confidential.
  • Use the portal exclusively for legitimate academic, administrative, and communication purposes.
  • Report any unauthorized access or suspicious activity to the school administration immediately.

3. Account & Access

  • The school reserves the right to suspend or restrict access in case of policy violations or misuse.
