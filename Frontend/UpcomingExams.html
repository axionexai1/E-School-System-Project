Upcoming Exams

Shows upcoming examination schedules in advance so students can prepare early and manage their study plans.

Exam Categories

  • First Term
  • Mid Term
  • Final Term
  • Practical Exams
  • Date Sheet

Each Exam Entry Shows

Field Description
Subject Displays the subject for which the exam will be conducted.
Date Shows the scheduled examination date.
Time Displays the exam starting time.
Exam Hall Shows the classroom or examination hall where the exam will take place.

Important Information

  • Students can view all upcoming exams in one place.
  • The latest Date Sheet is available for download.
  • Practical and theory examinations are listed separately.
  • Exam dates are updated whenever the school publishes a new schedule.
  • Students can prepare early by checking upcoming exams regularly.
